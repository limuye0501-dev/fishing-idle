# Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

_Last updated: October 31, 2026 · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 31 日_

## English

**Captain, Don't Come Back** ("the app") is an offline idle game. We designed it to collect nothing about you.

- **No personal data is collected.** The app does not ask for, collect, or transmit your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any other personal information.
- **Game progress stays on your device.** Your save file and settings (sound, music, haptics) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **No ads, analytics, or tracking.** The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.
- **Purchases.** The optional "Full Version" in-app purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether the purchase is active on your Apple ID.
- **Children.** Because no data is collected, the app is suitable for all ages.
- **Changes.** If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

## 简体中文

《船长别回来》（以下简称“本应用”）是一款离线放置游戏，我们的设计原则是不收集你的任何信息。

- **不收集个人信息。** 本应用不会索取、收集或上传你的姓名、邮箱、位置、通讯录、照片、设备标识符或其他任何个人信息。
- **游戏进度只保存在你的设备上。** 存档和设置（音效、音乐、触感）仅保存在本应用在你设备上的私有存储中，不会上传。删除应用即一并删除。
- **无广告、无统计、无追踪。** 本应用不含广告，不含第三方统计或崩溃上报 SDK，也不会跨应用或网站追踪你。
- **内购。** 可选的“完整版”内购完全由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，我们拿不到你的支付信息；本应用只向 App Store 查询该购买在你的 Apple ID 上是否有效。
- **儿童。** 由于不收集任何数据，本应用适合所有年龄段。
- **变更。** 如本政策有变更，我们会更新本页面及上方日期。
- **联系我们。** 如有疑问请发邮件至 **support@example.com**。
